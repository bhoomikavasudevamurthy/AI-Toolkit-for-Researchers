# Practical AI Use Cases

This document highlights real-world applications of AI tools across research, productivity, and workflow optimization.

## Research Acceleration

**Tool:** ChatGPT / Perplexity
**Use Case:** Quickly summarizing research papers and extracting key insights.
**Impact:** Reduces literature review time significantly.


## Knowledge Organization

**Tool:** Notion AI
**Use Case:** Structuring scattered notes into organized knowledge hubs.
**Impact:** Improves clarity and retrieval of information.


## Workflow Automation

**Tool:** n8n / Zapier
**Use Case:** Automating repetitive tasks such as alerts, reminders, and data movement.
**Impact:** Saves manual effort and minimizes human error.


## Computer Vision Applications

**Tool:** Roboflow
**Use Case:** Building datasets and deploying vision models for object detection.
**Impact:** Enables scalable visual inspection and smart monitoring systems.


More applied use cases will be added as I continue exploring modern technologies.
